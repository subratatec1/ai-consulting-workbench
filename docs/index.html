Client information stays under your control.

The hosted studio keeps engagement content in browser memory. It does not save your brief to a server or persist it in browser storage. Reloading clears it. Downloaded files are your responsibility. The hosting provider still receives ordinary web request metadata.

Local AI mode sends the brief, sources, and notes to your locally configured Ollama model only after you press “Draft with local AI.” Restricted classification is blocked. Confidential content requires an explicit approval checkbox. Detector checks are partial; do not rely on automatic redaction.
